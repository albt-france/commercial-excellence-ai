# Data sources

Three kinds of data are used, and every page of the report says which one it shows.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **REAL** | Downloaded from a public statistics provider (below), unchanged. |
| **ESTIMATE** | Calculated from REAL data with stated assumptions (market value). |
| **SIMULATED** | Generated by `src/generate_sales.py` (seed 42). Cordis is a private company and publishes no sales detail: **no Cordis data is used anywhere.** |

## REAL data

All files are downloaded by `src/get_public_data.py` into `data/raw/`; `data/raw/download_log.csv` keeps each URL and the download date (9 November 2026).

| Data | Provider, dataset, series code | Unit | Years used | File |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coronary angioplasties (PCI) by country | **OECD Health Statistics**, dataflow `OECD.ELS.HD:DSD_HEALTH_PROC@DF_SURG_PROC` (version 1.2), medical procedure **`CM36_TRS` "Transluminal coronary angioplasty"** (ICD-9-CM 36.01, 36.02, 36.05), mode of provision `_T` (inpatient + day case) | `PRC` procedures; `PRC_10P5PS` procedures per 100,000 persons | 2014 to latest year: **2024** for 17 countries, **2025** for Türkiye, **2023** for New Zealand | `oecd_surg_proc_CM36_TRS.csv` |
| Population | **World Bank** World Development Indicators, `SP.POP.TOTL` | persons | 2025 | `worldbank_SP.POP.TOTL.json` |
| GDP per capita | World Bank WDI, `NY.GDP.PCAP.CD` | current USD | 2025 | `worldbank_NY.GDP.PCAP.CD.json` |
| Current health expenditure per capita | World Bank WDI, `SH.XPD.CHEX.PC.CD` | current USD | 2024 (2023 for Switzerland, Belgium, Spain, Romania, Türkiye, Israel, Australia) | `worldbank_SH.XPD.CHEX.PC.CD.json` |

Cross-check: Eurostat publishes the same procedure (`hlth_co_proc2`, code `CM36_TRS`) but only up to 2021 and only for European countries, so the OECD series is used (later years, and Korea, Australia, New Zealand, Israel).

**Countries covered (19):** EMEA: Germany, Austria, Switzerland, France, Belgium, Netherlands, Italy, Spain, Portugal, United Kingdom,
Sweden, Poland, Czechia, Romania, Türkiye, Israel. APAC: Korea, Australia, New Zealand.
**Not covered:** Japan, China, India, Singapore and the Gulf states have no `CM36_TRS` series at the OECD, so they are out of scope rather than estimated.

Growth of procedures (`pci_cagr`) is measured from 2019, the last year before COVID, to the latest real year.

## ESTIMATE: market value

`market value = real PCI count x devices per PCI x price x country price index`, by country, product line and year (`data/clean/market_size.csv`).

| Product line (generic) | Devices per PCI (assumption) | Reference net price, Germany 2024 (assumption) | Yearly price erosion (assumption) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnostic catheters | 2.5 (includes diagnostic angiograms) | EUR 28 | -1% |
| Guidewires | 1.6 | EUR 65 | -1% |
| PTCA balloons | 1.8 | EUR 160 | -3% |
| Coronary stents | 1.5 | EUR 380 | -5% |
| Drug-coated balloons | 0.2 (market +12% a year) | EUR 750 | -4% |
| Peripheral devices | 0.35 (proxy, not an OECD count) | EUR 420 | -2% |

- Country price index = (health spending per capita / Germany's)^0.35, bounded to 0.45-1.25: richer health systems pay more, but less than proportionally.
- Years after the last real year are projected with the country's 2019-latest growth, capped between -2% and +6% a year.
- These assumptions are round, plausible orders of magnitude for a case study. They are not market research and should be replaced by IQVIA / company data in real use.

## SIMULATED data (`src/generate_sales.py`, seed 42)

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `products.csv` | 6 generic product lines (no brand or Cordis product names) |
| `accounts.csv` | 357 fictional hospitals ("Hospital FR-054"), 4 types |
| `sales.csv` | 67,015 rows: month x hospital x product line, units, net price, revenue, Jan 2023 - Oct 2026 |
| `targets.csv` | monthly targets 2024-2026 by country x product line = last year's actual x planned growth, phased like last year |

How the simulation is calibrated on the real market: each country has a market share (3-14% before product mix, giving 2-25% by line),
a share trend (gaining in Türkiye, Poland, Korea, Romania; losing in France), seasonality (August holidays in Europe, Lunar New Year
and Chuseok in Korea, January in Oceania), price erosion by line, and events built in on purpose to be found by the analysis:
a 2026 German stent tender (-10% price), competitor wins at 10 large hospitals from April-August 2026, 10 ordinary churns, 35 new hospitals since 2025.
`src/generate_sales.py` prints the unit share of market by country and line so the calibration can be checked.
